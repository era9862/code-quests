Flow chart and pesudocode 

Flowchart:
1. A word is choosen
2. Generate the blanks for the word
3. Set the diffculty (number of lives)
    3a. Easy = 10 lives
    3b. Medium = 7 lives
    3c. Hard = 5 lives
4. user guesses a letter
5a. If its wrong: player loses life
5b. If its right: replace the respected blanks to the guessed letter  
6. Repeat steps 4-5 until there are no more lives or word is guessed right
